Help & How-to

Welcome! My Records Collection is a private catalog for your Vinyl, CD, Cassette, and Digital records. Here's everything you need to get started.

1. Your account

  • Create an account on the sign-in page. The first account created becomes the administrator.
  • Your collection is private to you — other users cannot see it.
  • Open the account menu (top-right, your name) to sign out, view the disclaimer, or delete your account.

2. Adding records

  1. Click + Add Record.
  2. Enter the artist and album (required). Use 🔍 Lookup Info to auto-fill year, genre, and cover art from MusicBrainz, iTunes, or Deezer.
  3. Optionally set the format, condition, notes, and tags/shelves.
  4. Click Save Record.

You can also Import CSV from the Data ▾ menu to add many records at once.

3. Browsing & finding

  • Search by artist, album, or notes, and filter by genre, year, or format.
  • Switch between Grid and List views.
  • Click 🎲 Surprise Me to pick a random record to play.
  • Click a card to open the full detail view with track list, ratings, and history.

4. Ratings, plays & tags

  • Rate a record 1–5 stars from its detail view.
  • Click 💿 Mark as played on a card (or "Mark as Played" in the detail view) to log a listen — this feeds your History and streaks.
  • Add tags/shelves (e.g. favorites, to sell) to group records. Click a tag chip to filter by it.

5. Wishlist & history

  • 💝 Wishlist tracks records you want to buy, with target prices.
  • 📻 History shows what you've played, with stats and streaks.

6. Backups & your data

  • From Data ▾, choose Backup to download a full JSON copy of your collection, or Restore to import one (duplicates are skipped).
  • Keep your own backups — see the data & usage disclaimer.

7. Appearance

  • Use the 🖥️ Theme menu to choose Light, Dark, or System (follows your device setting).

8. Administrators

  • Admins get 👥 User management in the account menu to approve pending sign-ups, enable/disable users, change roles, and delete users (which purges their data).
  • Toggle open self-registration there once you're ready to let anyone sign up.

9. Deleting your account

Account menu → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and all of your data. Back up first if you want to keep a copy.
